# Quickstart — your first two hours

You are new to the crew, or you are the person setting it up. This is the whole onboarding.
Do it in one sitting. Nothing here needs a decision you cannot reverse.

---

## Hour 1 — set yourself up

### 1. Read two files (15 min)

- [THE-RULES.md](THE-RULES.md) — the nine non-negotiables
- [../01_Crew/CREW-CHARTER.md](../01_Crew/CREW-CHARTER.md) — what you are agreeing to with the other person

Skip the rest of the repo for now. You will pull playbooks in as you need them.

### 2. Make your folder (5 min)

```bash
cp -r 01_Crew/members/_TEMPLATE 01_Crew/members/<your-name>
```

Add yourself to [../01_Crew/ROSTER.md](../01_Crew/ROSTER.md).

### 3. Fill in PROFILE.md (15 min)

Your folder's `PROFILE.md`. This is not a resume — it is the short version your crew partner
needs to be useful to you: what you are targeting, what you are strong at, what you are weak at,
what your timeline is. Be honest about the weak parts. A crew that does not know where you are
soft cannot cover for it.

### 4. Build your target company list (25 min)

Open [../02_Playbooks/01-TARGET-COMPANY-LIST.md](../02_Playbooks/01-TARGET-COMPANY-LIST.md) and
work through it. Aim for **10–15 companies**. Not 50 — 50 is a wish list, and a wish list cannot
be worked.

Write them into your folder's `TARGET-COMPANIES.md`.

This is the single highest-value 25 minutes in this document. Everything downstream — who you
reach out to, which postings matter, who you follow on LinkedIn, which events you attend —
narrows to this list.

---

## Hour 2 — start the machine

### 5. Set your weekly numbers (10 min)

Your folder's `GOALS.md`. Pick numbers you will actually hit this week, not aspirational ones:

| Metric | Conservative start | Stretch |
|---|---|---|
| New outreach contacts | 5 | 10–15 |
| Applications | 5 | 10–20 |
| Coffee chats booked | 1 | 2–3 |
| LinkedIn posts | 1 | 2 |
| LinkedIn comments on target-company posts | 5 | 10 |

Missing your numbers in week one teaches you nothing except that you set them wrong.

### 6. Do your first outreach — today (30 min)

Do not read the whole outreach playbook first. Do this:

1. Pick **one** company from your list
2. LinkedIn search: that company + a role one step above yours. Filter to your school's alumni
   if you have that filter — shared-school is the highest-response opener there is
3. Open [../03_Templates/linkedin-connection-note.md](../03_Templates/linkedin-connection-note.md),
   take the first template, change three words
4. Send it
5. Log it in `04_Trackers/outreach.csv`

Then do two more. The first one is the hard one. It is 300 characters and the worst realistic
outcome is that a stranger ignores you.

### 7. Set up the fresh-jobs sweep (15 min)

[../06_Tools/README.md](../06_Tools/README.md). If Python setup is going to derail you today,
skip it — do the sweep manually with the LinkedIn "Past 24 hours" filter and come back to the
tool this weekend. Do not let tooling become the reason you did not apply to anything.

### 8. Book the weekly check-in (5 min)

Pick a recurring 30-minute slot with your crew partner. Put it in both calendars.
[../01_Crew/ACCOUNTABILITY-PROTOCOL.md](../01_Crew/ACCOUNTABILITY-PROTOCOL.md) has the format.

Then open [../02_Playbooks/00-WEEKLY-OPERATING-RHYTHM.md](../02_Playbooks/00-WEEKLY-OPERATING-RHYTHM.md)
and run the week.

## The honest expectation

Weeks 1–3 will feel like nothing is happening. Outreach has a lag — replies come days later,
coffee chats land the week after, referrals surface a month after that. The check-in exists
partly so that during those weeks somebody who is not you can look at the numbers and confirm
you are doing the work, even though the work has not paid yet.

Keep the numbers up. The results are downstream of the numbers, not of how it feels.
