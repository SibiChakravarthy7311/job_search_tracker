## The trade board

The reason a crew beats a solo search: you are each strong where the other is weak. Make it
explicit so it actually gets used instead of staying a nice idea.

| Skill | Who has it | Who needs it | Traded how |
|---|---|---|---|
| _(example)_ Cold outreach | A | B | A drafts B's first three emails, B sends them |
| _(example)_ Mock interviews | B | A | 45 min, alternating, every second Sunday |
