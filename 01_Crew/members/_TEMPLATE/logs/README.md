# Logs — <your name>

Free-form. Nothing here is required; keep what is useful to you.

Things worth keeping:

- **`interview-<company>-<round>.md`** — questions asked, what you said, what you would change.
  This compounds. By the fifth interview you have a personal question bank no prep site can
  match, because it is the actual questions from the actual companies you are targeting.
- **`chat-<company>-<initials>.md`** — coffee chat notes, written within 30 minutes while it is
  fresh. What they said about the team, what they are hiring for, who they suggested you talk to,
  and the personal detail that makes your follow-up in six weeks not read like a form letter.
- **`rejections.md`** — one running file. Date, company, stage, reason if given. Two purposes:
  it makes the pattern visible (always at the phone screen? always after the take-home?), and it
  shrinks each individual rejection to one line in a list, which is roughly its actual size.
- **`wins.md`** — every reply, every chat that went well, every callback. On the bad weeks this
  is the file to open. Keeping it feels unnecessary right up until the week it is not.
