# Member folders

One folder per crew member. **You edit only your own** — that is what keeps two people working
in the same repo from ever hitting a merge conflict on a Sunday night.

## Adding yourself

```bash
cp -r 01_Crew/members/_TEMPLATE 01_Crew/members/<your-name>
```

Then add a row to [../ROSTER.md](../ROSTER.md).

## What lives in yours

| File | What it is | How often you touch it |
|---|---|---|
| `PROFILE.md` | The short version of you your crew partner needs to be useful | Once, then quarterly |
| `TARGET-COMPANIES.md` | Your 10-15, tiered, with contact status | Weekly |
| `GOALS.md` | Weekly numbers, current focus, standing commitments | Every check-in |
| `logs/` | Anything you want to keep — interview debriefs, chat notes, rejections | Whenever |

## What does not go here

Contact details of people you are reaching out to, resumes with your home address, credentials.
See [../../CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Assume this repo goes public one day.
